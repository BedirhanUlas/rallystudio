FAQ

Questions you
probably have.

Everything you need to know before reaching out. Still not sure? Just ask.

Send a question

Are there long-term contracts?

+

No. Everything is month-to-month. Cancel or pause with 14 days notice. Annual billing saves you 20% if you want it, but it's never a requirement.

Who actually does the work?

+

Just me. I do the strategy, the content, the communication. No outsourcing to someone you've never met. If you're paying Rally, you're working with Han.

Do you use AI to create content?

+

Yes, I use AI tools. Same way a photographer uses Lightroom or a video editor uses Premiere, as a tool, not a replacement for thinking. Every piece of content that goes out gets reviewed and shaped by a real person who knows your business.

What if I only need one thing, like a website?

+

Totally fine. One-time projects are a big part of what we do. A website starts around $1,500 and goes up depending on scope. Reach out and I'll give you a straight quote.

How long before I see results?

+

Social usually picks up within the first month or two. SEO takes longer, often 3 to 6 months before you see real movement in rankings. I'll tell you what's realistic for your specific situation upfront, not give you a number to make you feel good.

Do you run Google or Meta ads?

+

Yes, as part of the Full Studio plan. You pay Google or Meta directly for the ad spend. We charge for the management side only. Minimum recommended ad budget is $300/month to see meaningful movement.

What industries do you work with?

+

Restaurants, fitness studios, retail shops, service businesses, e-commerce. If you're trying to grow online and want someone who actually gives a damn about your results, reach out. We'll figure out if it's a good fit.

Do you work with restaurants?

+

Yes, restaurants are one of our favorite clients to work with. We offer website design, QR digital menus, food photography, and Google Business setup. Reach out and we'll put together a custom quote.

Services

Rally Studio provides digital marketing, web design, social media management, SEO, and video production services to small businesses in the United States. All services are delivered on a month-to-month basis unless an annual plan is agreed upon in writing.

Payment

Monthly retainers are billed at the start of each billing cycle. One-time project fees are billed 50% upfront and 50% upon delivery. All payments are in USD. Late payments beyond 14 days may result in a pause of services.

Cancellation

You may cancel your monthly plan with 14 days written notice via email. No refunds are issued for the current billing period. Annual plans may be cancelled with a prorated refund minus a 10% processing fee.

Ownership

Upon full payment, you own all final deliverables (website files, graphics, video edits). Rally Studio retains the right to display completed work in its portfolio unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Limitation of Liability

Rally Studio is not liable for indirect losses related to our services. Our total liability is limited to the amount paid in the most recent billing period. We do not guarantee specific search rankings, follower counts, or revenue outcomes.
