FAQ

Questions you
probably have.

Are there long-term contracts?

+

No. All plans are month-to-month. You can cancel or pause anytime with 14 days notice. Annual billing is available at a 20% discount, but it's never required.

Who actually does the work?

+

Rally Studio is a boutique studio — you work directly with the founder. No outsourcing, no junior freelancers. Every strategy, piece of content, and communication comes from one person who is fully invested in your results.

Do you use AI to create content?

+

We use the best tools available — including modern AI platforms — to move faster and deliver more. Every strategy, creative decision, and piece of communication is driven by a real person who knows your business. The result: agency-quality work at a fraction of the cost.

What if I only need one thing, like a website?

+

Absolutely fine. We do one-time projects too. A website starts around $1,500–$4,000 depending on scope and complexity. Reach out and we'll give you a clear, no-pressure quote.

How long before I see results?

+

Social media engagement typically improves within 30–60 days. SEO results take longer — usually 3–6 months for meaningful ranking changes. We'll always be honest with you about realistic timelines for your specific situation.

Do you run Google or Meta ads?

+

Yes, but ad management is part of our Custom plan. Ad spend budget is separate (paid directly to Google/Meta) — we charge only for strategy and management. Minimum recommended ad budget is $300/month.

What industries do you work with?

+

Rally is built for small businesses — restaurants, fitness studios, retail shops, service businesses, e-commerce brands. If you're trying to grow your online presence and want someone who'll actually care about your results, reach out and let's see if we're a good fit.

Terms of Service

Last updated: May 2026

Services

Rally Studio provides digital marketing, web design, social media management, SEO, and video production services to small businesses in the United States. All services are delivered on a month-to-month basis unless an annual plan is agreed upon in writing.

Payment

Monthly retainers are billed at the start of each billing cycle. One-time project fees are billed 50% upfront and 50% upon delivery. All payments are in USD. Late payments beyond 14 days may result in a pause of services.

Cancellation

You may cancel your monthly plan with 14 days written notice via email. No refunds are issued for the current billing period. Annual plans may be cancelled with a prorated refund minus a 10% processing fee.

Ownership

Upon full payment, you own all final deliverables (website files, graphics, video edits). Rally Studio retains the right to display completed work in its portfolio unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Limitation of Liability

Rally Studio is not liable for indirect losses related to our services. Our total liability is limited to the amount paid in the most recent billing period. We do not guarantee specific search rankings, follower counts, or revenue outcomes.
