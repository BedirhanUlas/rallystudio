Questions

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes. Our corn tortillas are gluten-free, and so are most salsas. Chips share a fryer with flour items, so let us know if you have celiac disease.

How long does a pickup order take?

Usually about 15 minutes. You'll get a text when it's on the pickup shelf.

Can I order for a big group?

For more than 20 tacos, call ahead or use catering so the kitchen can plan for it.

Is there seating?

Twelve seats inside and a few picnic tables out front.

What this site does for a restaurant

Each part of this page has one job: turn a hungry person on a phone into an order.

  1. Open or closed is calculated live, so nobody drives over to a locked door.
  2. Pickup orders go straight to the kitchen tablet, without a delivery app taking a cut.
  3. The menu, hours and photos match the Google profile, which is where a lot of people look a restaurant up first.
  4. A short weekly video keeps Instagram, TikTok and Google fresh from one shoot a month.
  5. Catering gets its own section, because one office lunch can equal a week of walk-ins.
