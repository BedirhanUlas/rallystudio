Good to know

What if I need to cancel or I'm running late?

Text us at least 24 hours ahead and there's no fee. If you're more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten the service or move you.

Do you take a deposit?

Color services over two hours are held with a card. Nothing is charged unless you miss the appointment without notice.

I don't know which service I need.

Book the free consultation and tell us what you have in mind. We'll agree on the plan and the price before anything starts.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, a kids' cut (12 and under) is $30. Book it as a short cut and add a note.

What this site does for a salon

Every part of this page is built to fill empty chairs without more phone calls.

  1. Booking works at 11 pm, when a lot of people finally get around to it.
  2. Prices are public, so people book knowing the cost, with fewer surprises at the chair.
  3. First-visit clients get a clear, low-risk way in: the free consultation.
  4. One weekly transformation video feeds Instagram, TikTok and Google from a normal workday.
  5. Policies sit next to the booking, which cuts no-shows and awkward conversations.
