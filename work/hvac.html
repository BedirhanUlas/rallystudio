AC not cooling? Check this first.

Four quick questions. Plenty of "no cooling" calls turn out to be something you can fix yourself in a few minutes, and we'd rather tell you that than charge for a visit.

Smell burning, see sparks, or hear a loud buzzing from the outdoor unit? Turn the system off at the thermostat and call us now.

Question 1 of 4

Is the thermostat set to Cool, and below the room temperature?

Also check it has power. A blank screen often just means dead batteries.

Question 2 of 4

Are the AC breakers on in your electrical panel?

Look for switches labeled AC, Condenser or Air Handler that sit in the middle position.

Question 3 of 4

Has the air filter been changed in the last three months?

A clogged filter is the most common cause of weak airflow and frozen coils.

Question 4 of 4

Do you see ice on the copper lines, or water pooling near the indoor unit?

Check the pipes going into the outdoor unit and the area around your air handler.
